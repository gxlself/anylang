How do I add widgets?

On iPhone/iPad, touch and hold an empty area on the Home Screen, choose Edit / Add Widget, then find Cí Daily and choose a size. For Lock Screen widgets, touch and hold the Lock Screen, choose Customize and add a widget. On Mac (macOS 14+), secondary-click the desktop and choose Edit Widgets. On Watch, install the companion app, edit a compatible watch face and choose this app's complication; add the app to Smart Stack separately. Large and extra-large widgets require Pro. WidgetKit controls refresh timing, so changes may not appear immediately.

Why is pronunciation silent?

Turn on playback in the app's Settings, raise the device or media volume, and check the current audio output (Bluetooth headphones may still be connected). Ensure the relevant system voice is available; download a voice in the device's spoken-content/accessibility voice settings if necessary. Test pronunciation inside the app before retrying a widget. Speech uses Apple's voices, not recorded audio.

How does sync work?

Use the same iCloud account on your devices, allow this app to use iCloud, and enable sync in the app's Settings. Open each app and use Sync Now. Watch can sync directly when online. No iCloud account is required for offline use; the sync status may show unavailable instead. Turning sync off does not delete existing cloud records. Sync is free and does not depend on Pro.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the same App Store purchase account (which can differ from your iCloud account). On iPhone open Profile > Pro; on iPad/Mac open Settings > Pro; on Watch open the Pro page when shown. Select Restore Purchases and allow Apple's authentication if requested. Pro is a non-consumable one-time purchase, not a subscription. It is shared between the platforms of this app, but purchases in another language app do not unlock this one. If the store is unavailable, check your connection and try again; do not buy repeatedly.

Which levels and annotations are included?

The Library lists the books actually included in the app. Pro unlocks books marked Pro and additional widget/study modes; it is not a guarantee that the whole official examination syllabus is included. Readings, accent or grammar annotations vary by word. For a content correction, send the word, book and app version to support.
