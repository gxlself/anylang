Cí Daily Privacy Policy

Data Not Collected

Cí Daily has no developer account, backend, ads, tracking, analytics uploads or third-party telemetry. The developer does not collect your vocabulary study data. The app does not request a microphone, record speech, use IDFA or ask for tracking permission.

On your device

Bundled vocabulary is read-only. Learning events, daily plans, progress, favorites and preferences are stored on your devices, including a shared App Group used by the app and its widgets. Review statistics are calculated locally. The current anonymous-analytics preference does not send statistics to the developer.

Optional iCloud

When iCloud sync is enabled and available, learning events and daily plans use your private CloudKit database in iCloud.com.gxlself.ci; settings use Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This is not a public database or a developer-operated server. The developer cannot browse your private learning records. Without an iCloud account you can still study offline. Turning sync off stops future sync but does not erase existing cloud records.

System speech and notifications

Pronunciation uses Apple's AVSpeechSynthesizer and system voices. No microphone or developer speech service is used. A voice download, if needed, is handled by Apple. Optional daily reminders are local notifications, scheduled only after permission is granted. Apple Watch and Mac use the same local learning and optional sync model.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled by Apple's App Store and verified using StoreKit 2. We do not receive your payment card details. Each language app has its own Pro product. Apple's purchase services and iCloud are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Deletion

To remove local data, remove widgets and delete the app and its local data from each device. On Mac, uninstalling the application may leave sandbox or App Group data; contact support for the exact locations before deleting files. To remove cloud data, first disable sync on all devices, then remove this app's data from system iCloud storage management if it is listed. Keep sync off until local copies have been removed so they are not uploaded again. Backups are separate. We cannot delete your private iCloud records on your behalf.

Support and this website

If you email gxlself@gmail.com, we see the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Do not send payment details or other sensitive information. This website uses no analytics scripts or cookies added by the developer. GitHub Pages may process hosting request logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

This policy covers the iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and widget versions of Cí Daily. If the data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated. Contact: gxlself@gmail.com.
